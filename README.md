### Part II: Medieval Indian History (Chapters 8–13)

### Module 8: Early Medieval India (650 AD – 1206 AD) &bull; `early_medieval.html`
- **Tripartite Struggle for Kannauj:** Gurjara-Pratiharas (Nagabhata, Mihir Bhoja), Palas of Bengal (Dharmapala - Vikramashila University), and Rashtrakutas of Deccan (Dantidurga, Krishna I - Kailasa rock temple at Ellora, Amoghavarsha - *Kavirajamarga*).
- **Rajput Dynasties:** Chauhans of Ajmer (Prithviraj III), Chandellas of Bundelkhand (Khajuraho temples), Paramaras of Malwa (Raja Bhoj), Solankis of Gujarat (Modhera Sun temple).
- **Foreign Arab & Turkish Invasions:** Muhammad bin Qasim (712 AD conquest of Sindh), Mahmud of Ghazni (17 raids, Somnath 1025 AD, Al-Biruni's *Kitab-ul-Hind*), Muhammad Ghori (Battles of Tarain 1191 & 1192 AD, Battle of Chandawar 1194 AD).
- **The Imperial Cholas (850–1279 AD):** Vijayalaya, Rajaraja I (Brihadisvara Temple at Thanjavur), Rajendra I (*Gangaikondachola*, maritime conquest of Srivijaya / Sumatra), Uttaramerur Inscription (local village autonomy and Kudavolai ballot system).

### Module 9: The Delhi Sultanate (1206 AD – 1526 AD) &bull; `delhi_sultanate.html`
- **The Slave / Mamluk Dynasty (1206–1290 AD):** Qutbuddin Aibak (*Lakhbaksh*, Qutb Minar, Adhai Din ka Jhopra), Shamsuddin Iltutmish (Turkan-i-Chahalgani, Iqtadari system, silver *Tanka* & copper *Jital*), Razia Sultan (first female sovereign), Ghiyasuddin Balban (Blood & Iron policy, *Sajda & Paibos*, Nauroz festival, Diwan-i-Arz).
- **The Khalji Dynasty (1290–1320 AD):** Jalaluddin Khalji, Alauddin Khalji (Market Control Regulations, *Shahna-i-Mandi*, *Dagh* branding & *Chehra* descriptive rolls, Alai Darwaza, Malik Kafur's southern campaigns).
- **The Tughlaq Dynasty (1320–1414 AD):** Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq (Tughlaqabad), Muhammad bin Tughlaq (5 ambitious schemes: Doab taxation, Daulatabad capital transfer, token copper/brass currency, Khurasan & Qarachil expeditions, Ibn Battuta's *Rihla*), Firoz Shah Tughlaq (canal network, Jizya on Brahmins, *Diwan-i-Khairat*, *Diwan-i-Bandagan*), and Timur's 1398 AD invasion of Delhi.
- **The Sayyid (1414–1451 AD) & Lodi (1451–1526 AD) Dynasties:** Khizr Khan, Bahlol Lodi, Sikandar Lodi (founded Agra in 1504 AD, *Gaz-i-Sikandari* measurement), Ibrahim Lodi (defeated by Babur at First Battle of Panipat 1526 AD).

### Module 10: Vijayanagar & Bahmani Kingdoms (1336 AD – 1646 AD) &bull; `vijayanagar_bahmani.html`
- **The Vijayanagar Empire:** Founded by Harihara I & Bukka I on Tungabhadra banks. Four Dynasties: Sangama, Saluva, Tuluva, Aravidu.
- **Sri Krishnadeva Raya (1509–1529 AD):** Golden age, *Amuktamalyada* (Telugu), Ashtadiggajas (Allasani Peddana, Tenali Rama), Vittala Temple Stone Chariot, Hazara Rama Temple. Accounts of foreign travellers Domingo Paes, Duarte Barbosa, Nicolo de Conti, and Abdur Razzaq.
- **Battle of Talikota / Rakshasi-Tangadi (1565 AD):** Destruction of Vijayanagar by Deccan Sultanates alliance.
- **Administration & Economy:** *Nayankara* system (military feudalism) and *Ayagar* system (12 village functionaries). Gold coinage (*Varaha / Pagoda*).
- **The Bahmani Kingdom & 5 Deccan Sultanates:** Alauddin Hasan Bahman Shah (Gulbarga capital), Firoz Shah Bahmani, Mahmud Gawan (Bidar madrasa). Breakup into: Imad Shahi (Berar), Adil Shahi (Bijapur - Gol Gumbaz Whispering Gallery), Nizam Shahi (Ahmadnagar - Chand Bibi), Qutb Shahi (Golconda - Charminar & diamonds), Barid Shahi (Bidar).

### Module 11: The Mughal Empire (1526 AD – 1707 AD) &bull; `mughal_empire.html`
- **Babur (1526–1530 AD):** Panipat (1526), Khanwa (1527), Chanderi (1528), Ghaghra (1529); *Tuzuk-i-Baburi* (Baburnama in Chagatai Turkic).
- **Humayun (1530–1540, 1555–1556 AD):** Battles of Chausa (1539) and Kannauj/Bilgram (1540); 15-year exile; restoration; *Humayunama* by Gulbadan Begum.
- **Sur Empire / Sher Shah Suri (1540–1545 AD):** Grand Trunk Road (G.T. Road), silver *Rupiya* & copper *Dam*, *Patta & Qabuliyat* land deeds, Sasaram mausoleum.
- **Akbar the Great (1556–1605 AD):** Second Battle of Panipat (1556, Bairam Khan vs Hemu), Battle of Haldighati (1576, Maharana Pratap), Mansabdari System (Zat & Sawar), Todar Mal's Dahsala/Zabti system, Ibadat Khana, Din-i-Ilahi (1582), Buland Darwaza at Fatehpur Sikri.
- **Jahangir (1605–1627 AD):** Chain of Justice (*Zanjir-i-Adl*), British envoys Captain William Hawkins and Sir Thomas Roe, Shalimar Bagh, zenith of Mughal portrait painting (Ustad Mansur, Abul Hasan).
- **Shah Jahan (1628–1658 AD):** Golden Age of Mughal Architecture (Taj Mahal, Red Fort, Jama Masjid, Peacock Throne *Takht-i-Taus*).
- **Aurangzeb Alamgir (1658–1707 AD):** Re-imposition of Jizya (1679 AD), annexations of Bijapur (1686) and Golconda (1687), 25-year Deccan quagmire, Bibi ka Maqbara.

### Module 12: The Maratha Empire & Later Mughals (1674 AD – 1818 AD) &bull; `marathas.html`
- **Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj (1674–1680 AD):** Guerrilla warfare (*Ganimi Kava*), conquest of Torna, Kondana (Sinhagad), Purandar; encounters with Afzal Khan and Shaista Khan; Treaty of Purandar (1665 AD) with Raja Jai Singh; escape from Agra (1666 AD); grand coronation (*Chhatrapati*) at Raigad (1674 AD).
- **Maratha Administration:** *Ashtapradhan* (8 ministers: Peshwa, Amatya, Sachiv, Mantri, Senapati, Sumant, Nyayadhish, Panditrao); *Chauth* (25%) and *Sardeshmukhi* (10% additional levy); Kathi land measurement.
- **The Peshwa Supremacy (1713–1818 AD):** Balaji Vishwanath (First Peshwa), Baji Rao I (Hindu Pad-Padshahi, undefeated in 40 battles), Balaji Baji Rao (Nana Saheb).
- **Third Battle of Panipat (14 January 1761):** Sadashivrao Bhau and Viswasrao vs. Ahmad Shah Abdali.
- **The Later Mughals & Foreign Invasions:** Bahadur Shah I, Farrukhsiyar (Golden Farman 1717 to British EIC), Muhammad Shah Rangeela (Nadir Shah's 1739 sack of Delhi and loot of Koh-i-Noor & Peacock Throne), Shah Alam II (Battle of Buxar 1764, Treaty of Allahabad 1765), Bahadur Shah Zafar (last emperor exiled to Rangoon 1857).

### Module 13: Religious Movements: Bhakti & Sufi Traditions (15th–16th c.) &bull; `bhakti_sufi.html`
- **Philosophical Revival & 5 Vedantic Schools:** Shankaracharya (Advaita), Ramanuja (Vishishtadvaita), Madhvacharya (Dvaita), Nimbarkacharya (Dvaitadvaita / Bhedabheda), Vallabhacharya (Shuddhadvaita & Pushti Marg). Ramananda's bridge from South to North India.
- **Nirguna vs. Saguna Saints:** Sant Kabir (Varanasi weaver, *Bijak*, *Dohas*), Guru Nanak (founder of Sikhism, *Langar & Sangat*), Dadu Dayal (*Dadu Panth*); Saguna icons: Mirabai (Merta princess, Krishna devotion), Chaitanya Mahaprabhu (Gaudiya Vaishnavism, *Sankirtan*), Surdas (*Sursagar*), Tulsidas (*Ramcharitmanas* in Awadhi), Purandar Dasa (Pitamaha of Carnatic music), Thyagaraja.
- **Maharashtra Dharma & Varkari Movement:** Jnaneshwar (*Jnaneshwari / Bhavartha Dipika*), Namadeva (tailor, Vithoba of Pandharpur, Varkari pilgrimage, verses in Guru Granth Sahib), Eknath (*Bhavartha Ramayana*), Tukaram (*Abhangas*), Samarth Ramdas (*Dasbodha*).
- **The Sufi Movement & 4 Silsilas:** Khwaja Ali Hujwiri (*Kashf-ul-Mahjub*); Chishti Order (Muinuddin Chishti of Ajmer, Bakhtiyar Kaki, Baba Farid, Nizamuddin Auliya, Chirag-i-Delhi, Gesu Daraz); Suhrawardi Order (Bahauddin Zakariya of Multan); Qadiri Order (Mian Mir); Naqshbandi Order (Sheikh Ahmad Sirhindi *Mujaddid Alif Sani*). Core terms: *Pir*, *Murid*, *Khanqah*, *Sama*, *Fana*, *Ziyarat*.
