## 🌟 Modules Complete (Ancient Indian History 100% Covered)

### Module 1: Harappan / Indus Valley Civilization (2500 BC – 1750 BC)
- **Interactive Geographical Compass:** Manda (North), Daimabad (South), Alamgirpur (East), Sutkagendor (West).
- **Authentic Relics Gallery:** Bronze Dancing Girl, Priest-King, Pashupati Seal, Great Bath, Lothal Dockyard, and Dholavira Reservoirs.
- **Major Archaeological Sites Explorer:** Harappa, Mohenjodaro, Chanhudaro, Lothal, Kalibangan, Dholavira, Banawali, Surkotada, and Rakhigarhi.
- **Economic Trade Matrix:** Sumerian trade route (*Meluhha ➔ Dilmun ➔ Makan ➔ Sumer/Akkad*) and raw material import sources.
- **Theories of Decline:** Comparison between Wheeler, Marshall/Rao, D.P. Agrawal, and Fairservis.
- **Interactive Flashcards & 5-Question Mock Quiz.**

### Module 2: Vedic Culture (1500 BC – 600 BC)
- **Origins & Aryan Homeland Theories:** Max Müller (Central Asia), B.G. Tilak (Arctic), Swami Dayanand Saraswati (Tibet), and the **Boghazkai Inscription (1400 BC)**.
- **The Four Vedas & Vedatrayi:** Rig Veda (1028 hymns, Gayatri Mantra, Dasrajan War, Purusha Sukta), Sama Veda (Origin of Indian music), Yajur Veda (Verse & Prose rituals), and Atharva Veda.
- **Philosophical Canon:** Brahmanas (*Shatapatha*), Upanishads (*Mundaka - "Satyameva Jayate"*, *Brihadaranyaka*, *Katha*, *Jabala*), 6 Vedangas, and Shad-Darshanas.
- **Nadisukta Rigvedic Rivers:** Ancient Vedic river names vs. Modern equivalents (*Sindhu, Saraswati, Parushni, Vitasta, Asikni, Vipas, Shutudri, Kubha*).
- **Early Vedic vs. Later Vedic Matrix:** Evolution of Polity, Society, Economy (Iron / *Shyama Ayas* discovery at Atranjikhera), and Religion.
- **Interactive Flashcards & 5-Question Mock Quiz.**

### Module 3: 16 Mahajanapadas & Magadha Empire (600 BC – 325 BC)
- **16 Mahajanapadas Full Matrix:** Anga, Magadha, Vajji, Malla, Kasi, Kosala, Vatsa, Chedi, Kuru, Panchala, Matsya, Surasena, Ashmaka (only Dakshinapatha Mahajanapada), Avanti, Gandhara, Kamboja with ancient & modern capitals.
- **Rise of Magadha Imperialism:** Haryanka Dynasty (Bimbisara, Ajatashatru), Shishunaga Dynasty (Kalasoka), and Nanda Dynasty (Mahapadma Nanda *Ekarat*, Dhanananda).
- **Foreign Invasions:** Persian / Achaemenid invasion (Cyrus, Darius I, Kharoshthi script diffusion) and Macedonian invasion (Alexander the Great, Battle of Hydaspes 326 BC against King Porus).
- **Interactive Flashcards & 5-Question Mock Quiz.**

### Module 4: Religious Movements: Buddhism & Jainism (600 BC – 400 BC)
- **Gautama Buddha & Buddhism:** Great Events (*Mahabhinishkramana*, *Dharmachakrapravartana*, *Mahaparinirvana*), Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path (*Ashtangika Marga*), 3 Pitakas, and Hinayana vs. Mahayana.
- **The 4 Great Buddhist Councils:** 1st at Rajgriha (Ajatashatru), 2nd at Vaishali (Kalasoka), 3rd at Pataliputra (Ashoka), and 4th at Kundalvana (Kanishka).
- **Vardhamana Mahavira & Jainism:** 24 Tirthankaras (Rishabhadeva, Parshvanatha, Mahavira), Triratna (*Samyak Darshana, Jnana, Charitra*), 5 Vows, Syadvada / Anekantavada, Digambaras vs. Shvetambaras.
- **The 2 Jain Councils:** 1st at Pataliputra (Sthulabhadra) and 2nd at Vallabhi (Devardhi Kshamasramana).
- **Interactive Flashcards & 5-Question Mock Quiz.**

### Module 5: The Mauryan Empire (322 BC – 185 BC)
- **Dynastic Sovereigns:** Chandragupta Maurya (Chanakya's Arthashastra, defeat of Seleucus, Megasthenes' *Indica*, Sallekhana at Shravanabelagola), Bindusara (*Amitraghata*, Greek envoys Deimachus & Dionysius), Ashoka the Great (Kalinga War 261 BC, Dhamma-Vijaya, Upagupta), and Brihadratha.
- **Ashoka's 14 Major Rock Edicts (MRE):** Complete breakdown from Ahimsa (MRE I) to Kalinga War & 5 Hellenistic contemporary kings (MRE XIII).
- **Administrative Machinery:** Saptanga theory of state and 8 key ministerial superintendents (*Samaharta*, *Sannidhata*, *Sitadhyaksha*, *Vyavaharika*, *Pradeshta*, *Sanstha & Sanchara*).
- **Megasthenes' Account:** 7 castes and 6 municipal boards (30 commissioners) of Pataliputra.
- **Mauryan Art & Architecture:** Sanchi Stupa, Didarganj Yakshi, Barabar Caves (Lomas Rishi), and Ashokan monolithic pillars & Lion Capital.
- **Interactive Flashcards & 5-Question Mock Quiz.**

### Module 6: Post-Mauryan & Sangam Age (185 BC – 319 AD)
- **Native Successor Dynasties:** Shungas (Pushyamitra, Patanjali's *Mahabhashya*, Heliodorus Besnagar Garuda Pillar), Kanvas (Vasudeva), Satavahanas (Simuka, Gautamiputra Satakarni *Ekabrahmana*, Hala's *Gatha Saptasati*, Nanaghat land grants), and Chedis (Kharavela, Hathigumpha inscription).
- **Foreign Dynasties:** Indo-Greeks (Menander, *Milindapanho*, first gold coins), Shakas (Rudradaman I, Junagadh Sanskrit inscription 150 AD, Sudarshana lake repairs, Vikrama Samvat 58 BC), Parthians (Gondophernes), Kushanas (Kanishka I, Saka Era 78 AD, Charaka, Ashvaghosha, Nagarjuna).
- **Gandhara Art vs. Mathura Art:** Complete comparative matrix (Greco-Roman blue-grey schist vs. indigenous spotted red sandstone).
- **The Sangam Age:** Cheras (Bow, Senguttuvan & Pattini cult), Cholas (Tiger, Karikala & Grand Anicut), Pandyas (Fish, Madurai); 3 Sangams; Epics: *Silappadikaram*, *Manimekalai*, and *Tirukkural*.
- **Interactive Flashcards & 5-Question Mock Quiz.**

### Module 7: The Gupta Golden Age & Harshavardhana (319 AD – 647 AD)
- **The Imperial Guptas:** Chandragupta I (Gupta Era 319–320 AD, *Maharajadhiraja*), Samudragupta (Napoleon of India, Harisena's *Prayag Prashasti*, Veena gold coins), Chandragupta II Vikramaditya (Navaratnas, Fa-Hien, Mehrauli Iron Pillar), Kumaragupta I (Nalanda University foundation), Skandagupta (Huna repulse, Sudarshana lake restoration), and Eran Inscription 510 AD (earliest epigraphic Sati record).
- **Science, Mathematics & Astronomy:** Aryabhata (*Aryabhatiya*, earth rotation, eclipses, &pi; calculation), Varahamihira (*Panchasiddhantika*, *Brihatsamhita*), Brahmagupta (gravitation anticipation, zero as number), Dhanvantari (Ayurveda).
- **Classical Sanskrit Literature:** Kalidasa (*Abhijnanashakuntalam*, *Meghaduta*), Visakhadatta (*Mudrarakshasa*), Shudraka (*Mrichchhakatika*), Vishnu Sharma (*Panchatantra*), Amarasimha (*Amarakosha*).
- **Monuments & Art:** Nalanda Mahavihara ruins, Mehrauli rustless iron pillar, Ajanta cave frescoes (Caves 16 & 17), and earliest structural stone temples (Dashavatara Temple at Deogarh).
- **Emperor Harshavardhana (606–647 AD):** Capital at Kannauj, conflict with Pulakeshin II (Aihole Inscription 634 AD), Hiuen Tsang (*Si-Yu-Ki*), Banabhatta (*Harshacharita*, *Kadambari*), and royal dramas (*Ratnavali*, *Priyadarshika*, *Nagananda*).
- **Interactive Flashcards & 5-Question Mock Quiz.**

## 🎨 Interactive Features Across All Modules

- 🌓 **White / Dark Theme Toggle:** Instant persistence using `localStorage`.
- 🔍 **Interactive Image Lightbox:** Drag-to-pan and mouse-wheel zoom up to 400% on historical artifacts, maps, coins, and edicts.
- ⚡ **Instant Search Modal:** Hotkey `ESC` / click-to-search across all prelims facts, rulers, texts, and terms.
- 🗂️ **3D Active Recall Flashcards:** Flippable flashcards testing exact Lucent prelims memory triggers.
- 📝 **5-Question Mock Quizzes:** Real-time scoring, option feedback, and detailed explanatory commentary.
- 🧭 **Cross-Chapter Navigation:** Seamless dropdown selector and chronological breadcrumb footer links.
