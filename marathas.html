Chapter 12 • 1674 AD – 1818 AD • Swarajya & The Fall of Empires

The Maratha Empire

Founded by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj through revolutionary hill guerrilla warfare (*Ganimi Kava*), the Maratha Empire challenged Mughal hegemony and instituted the celebrated Ashtapradhan council. Expanded into a pan-Indian confederacy under Peshwa Baji Rao I, its expansion peaked until the tragic carnage of the Third Battle of Panipat (1761 AD).

Maratha Sovereign

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj • 1627–1680 AD

Founder of Hindavi Swarajya; British Museum contemporary painting depicting the Maratha liberator.

The Life of a Liberator

Chhatrapati Shivaji's Rise & Treaties

Lucent GK essentials covering forts, the Afzal Khan episode, Purandar treaty, and Raigad coronation.

Birth & Mentors 1627 AD

Origins & Youth

Born at Shivneri Fort (Pune) to Shahji Bhonsle and Jijabai. Spiritual mentor was Samarth Ramdas; guardian-administrator was Dadaji Kondadeo.

First Conquest (1646):

Captured the strategic fort of Torna at age 19, followed by Raigad and Sinhagad.

Milestones 1659 & 1663

Afzal Khan & Shaista Khan

Slew Bijapur general Afzal Khan at Pratapgarh in 1659 using concealed *Bagh Nakh* (tiger claws) when Khan attempted an ambush.

Lal Mahal Surprise (1663):

Daring night raid on Mughal viceroy Shaista Khan inside Pune, severing his thumb and routing his guards.

Treaty of Purandar June 1665 AD

Purandar & Agra Escape

Signed treaty with Raja Jai Singh I of Amber. Shivaji surrendered 23 of his 35 forts (yielding 4 lakh huns) to Aurangzeb.

Agra Court Escape (1666):

Insulted by Aurangzeb at court; placed under house arrest; escaped miraculously concealed inside large sweet baskets.

Coronation 6 June 1674 AD

Chhatrapati at Raigad

Solemn coronation conducted at Raigad Fort by celebrated scholar Gaga Bhatt of Varanasi.

Royal Titles:

Assumed the titles of Chhatrapati and Haindava Dharmoddharak (Protector of Hindu Faith).

Chauth (One-Fourth / 25%) 25% Assessment

A military protection levy equivalent to one-fourth (25%) of standard land revenue paid by neighboring Mughal and Deccan territories to ensure immunity from Maratha raids and protection against foreign aggression.

Sardeshmukhi (One-Tenth / 10%) 10% Surcharge

An additional levy of one-tenth (10%) demanded by Shivaji on the basis of his legal claim as the hereditary supreme head (*Sardesmukh*) of all Deshmukhs in Maharashtra.

The Prime Minister Sovereign

The Peshwa Era (1713–1818 AD)

Balaji Vishwanath, Baji Rao I, and the catastrophic watershed of Panipat (1761 AD).

1713–1720 AD First Peshwa

Balaji Vishwanath

Made the office of Peshwa hereditary. Secured the historic 1719 treaty from the Sayyid brothers recognizing Shahu as sovereign of Swarajya with rights to collect Chauth across the Deccan.

1720–1740 AD 40 Battles Undefeated

Baji Rao I (Greatest Cavalry General)

Championed Hindu Pad-Padshahi. Smashed the Nizam of Hyderabad at the Battle of Palkhed (1728) and Battle of Bhopal (1737); raided the gates of Delhi in 1737 with 500 cavalrymen.

14 January 1761 Panipat III

Third Battle of Panipat

Fought between Marathas under Sadashivrao Bhau and the Afghan king Ahmad Shah Abdali. Marathas were annihilated: "Two pearls were dissolved, 27 gold mohurs lost...".
